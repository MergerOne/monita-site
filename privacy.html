Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Short version: we don’t collect anything. Monita has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Times and dates are read on your device, smart features use Apple’s on-device model or Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, your reminders sync through your own iCloud to the people you invite, and we never receive any of your content.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your reminders, lists, notes and history are stored on your devices and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud database (Apple’s CloudKit). Reminder text is stored encrypted there. We have no access to your iCloud and can’t read it.
  • Reading times and dates in your sentences (“dentist Friday 3pm”) happens on your device. The sentence is never sent to us.
  • Smart features (on devices with Apple Intelligence: finding reminders in text you share, suggesting who to send a reminder to, answering questions about your history, gentle hints, and picking an icon): the text involved is processed by Apple Private Cloud Compute, Apple’s privacy-built cloud model. Apple uses it only to answer that request and does not keep it, and we never receive it. These features need a network connection and have a daily limit. To keep everything on your device, turn on Process on This Device Only in Monita’s settings; the app then uses Apple’s on-device model where available, or its built-in reader.
  • Voice input (only when you tap the microphone): your speech is turned into text on your device. Audio is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos in notes (only when you attach one to a reminder): the photo is stored with that reminder on your devices and in your own iCloud. If the reminder is in a group, the people in that group can see the photo.
  • Photos (only when you pick one, for a background or to make a reminder from text in a photo): the photo is read on your device. A background photo is kept on the device; photos used for text are not kept.
  • Location (only if you add a reminder for arriving at or leaving a place): the app asks for location access only while you use it, and uses Apple’s system to notice when you arrive or leave. Your location and your saved places stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Weather (only if you turn on weather): to show the forecast, the app sends your location to Apple’s WeatherKit service and gets the weather back. The request goes straight to Apple, not to us; we never see or store your location. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.
  • Health (only if you turn on “Complete from Workouts” for a reminder): the app reads workouts from the Health app on your iPhone to mark that reminder as completed. It never writes to Health. When a workout completes a reminder, only the workout type (for example “walking”) is saved with that reminder, so you (and the group, for a group reminder) can see what completed it; it syncs through your iCloud like the rest of the reminder. Duration, distance, route, heart rate and all other workout data stay on your iPhone and are never stored in iCloud or sent to us.
  • Calendar (only if you turn on Calendar Events): the app reads your events to show them next to your reminders. It never changes your calendar and never sends events anywhere.
  • Settings are stored on your device.

Groups

When you create or join a group, the reminders sent in that group (with their notes and any attached photos), your display name in the app, and who has completed what are shared only with the people in that group, through Apple’s iCloud sharing. Apple handles the sharing; we can’t see it.

To make joining easier, an invite can come with a 6-digit invite code. The code is stored in the app’s public iCloud database together with the invite link and an expiry time, and nothing else: no names, no reminders. It stops working when it expires or when you withdraw the invite.

You can leave a group, or remove people from a group you created, at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We don’t receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks on your device whether a plan is active.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don’t have any.

This website

usemonita.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Deleting your data

Delete a reminder and it’s deleted on every device it synced to. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove the iCloud copy: Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Monita › Delete Data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
