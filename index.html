Private by design

Monita doesn’t receive your reminders.

Monita has no servers, no sign-up and no analytics. Here is where your reminders go. Read the full Privacy Policy.

  • On your deviceTimes and dates in your sentences, your voice, and photos you pick are read on your iPhone. Your location stays there too.
  • Through your iCloudReminders, with their notes, photos and places, sync through your own iCloud to your devices and to the people in your groups.
  • Optional Apple IntelligenceOn supported devices, smart features use Apple Private Cloud Compute. Apple doesn’t keep the text. To keep it on your device, turn on Settings › Apple Intelligence › Process on This Device Only.

No ads, no analytics, no third-party code. This website has no cookies or trackers either.

Questions

Do the people I invite have to pay?

No. People you invite can always receive, check off and answer reminders for free. With a Group plan, everyone in your group gets the full app.

Do I need an account?

No Monita account is needed. Sync and groups use your own iCloud, and Monita has no servers.

Does Monita work on Android?

Not today. Monita runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and everyone in a group needs an Apple device.

Can a reminder ring like an alarm?

Yes. Turn on Ring Like an Alarm for a reminder you can’t miss. On iOS 26.1 or later, once you allow Monita to use alarms, it rings through silent mode and Focus. On earlier versions it plays a longer ringtone, which the silent switch still mutes.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Reminders you’ve already set keep ringing. You can still complete, stop and delete them. Creating new ones needs an active plan.
