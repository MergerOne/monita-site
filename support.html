Common questions

My reminder didn’t ring.

Check that Settings › Notifications › Monita is allowed. If a Focus is on, make sure Time Sensitive notifications are allowed for Monita. For something you can’t miss, turn on Ring Like an Alarm for that reminder.

Reminders don’t show up on my iPad.

Both devices need the same Apple Account, with iCloud turned on for Monita (Settings › [your name] › iCloud).

Someone didn’t get the reminder I sent.

They need Monita installed, notifications allowed, and to have joined your group. Groups work between Apple devices only.

I invited someone, but they can’t join.

Invites expire. In Groups, send a new invite or a new code. If the link opened in a browser instead of the app, copy the whole message, open Groups in Monita, and tap Paste.

The time was read wrong.

Tap the time before adding to change it, or rewrite the sentence. Send us the sentence and we’ll teach Monita to read it.

How do I manage or cancel my plan?

Open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. If your plan ends, reminders you’ve already set keep ringing.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app to remove the data on that device. To remove the iCloud copy: Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Monita › Delete Data.
